About

Sixty-six million years ago something ancient ended and something new began. Astrology is the study of those long cycles — the slow grammar of sky and time. I read charts the way paleontologists read stone: patiently, with attention to what is buried and what is about to surface.

Blog

Dispatches from the long arc of the planets. Notes on transits, seasons, and the particular quality of light in a given week. New entries when the sky says something worth writing down.

Services

Natal chart readings, transit forecasts, and synastry sessions. Each reading is built from your full chart — no automated reports, no generalities. Booking opens in small windows. Get in touch to find out when.

Contact

Questions, bookings, or just something you want to say about Saturn — reach out. Response time varies with the season.
